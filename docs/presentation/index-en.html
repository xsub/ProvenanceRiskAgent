PR Decision model
Three independent dimensions

No findings does not mean safe when the investigation lacks evidence

01

Risk

How severe are the deterministic findings, and what is their combined weight?

Answers: “what did we find?”
02

Completeness

Are the required evidence categories present, and did the investigation cover the right scope?

Answers: “what did we not check?”
03

Confidence

How much can we trust the result after accounting for gaps, contradictions, and source failures?

Answers: “how certain is the verdict?”
Low risk + low completeness ≠ evidence of safety. Such a case routes to UNKNOWN or REVIEW.
